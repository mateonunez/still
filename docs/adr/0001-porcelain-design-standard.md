# ADR 0001: Porcelain as the initial design standard

Status: Accepted on 2026-10-04.

## Context

The discovery prototype compared Porcelain, Spectrum, and Meadow. Porcelain is the initial light/dark standard. Other directions remain candidates for a future theme collection or marketplace.

## Decision

Use Porcelain as the initial product design standard. Light and dark appearances share the same layout, typography, behavior, and information hierarchy. The current brand refinement uses burgundy on warm porcelain in light mode, and deep plum with ivory/blush in dark mode. See the canonical tokens and brand fundamentals. Appearance is distinct from a wallpaper/theme family.

Keep Spectrum and Meadow as design exploration assets. Future collections and a possible marketplace are roadmap opportunities; neither is a dependency of the first release.

## Consequences

The first native UI should prioritize Porcelain and consistent appearance support. This decision does not approve native implementation, marketplace infrastructure, payments, distribution, or the product name. The browser prototype is a review artifact; native behavior still requires its own verification.
